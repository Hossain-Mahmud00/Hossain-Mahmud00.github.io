Inputs

Method: log-mean temperature difference, tube side by Dittus–Boelter, condensing side by the Nusselt film correlation for a horizontal tube, with wall and fouling resistances. Tubes are ¾ inch (19.05 mm OD, 15.75 mm ID) stainless steel.

This is a first-pass estimate. A real tube bundle usually condenses less efficiently than one tube, and the shell diameter and condensing-side pressure drop are not designed here.

TCS latent heat and liquid properties are held at their values near 305 K, so results drift away from that temperature. All inputs are examples, not plant data.
